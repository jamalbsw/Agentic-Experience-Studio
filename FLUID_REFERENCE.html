Glass Effects & Cards

The foundation of our visual language is the translucent liquid glass effect. All cards and panels use this styling.

Example Card with Glass Effect

This is a card with the full Fluid Intelligence glass effect. Notice the subtle gradient, backdrop blur, and rim lighting.

The warm coral accent (#C7836F) is used here for important elements representing human judgment.

Best Practices

DO ✓

Use Liquid Azure (#4881AA) for all interactive elements and titles
Apply glass effects to all cards and panels consistently
Use cyan glow (#B9EAF5) for focus indicators and active states
Reserve warm coral (#C7836F) for human judgment and decisions
Test focus states with keyboard navigation
Use clamp() for responsive sizing

DON'T ✗

Use white borders anywhere (#FFFFFF or rgba(255,255,255,*))
Use outdated purple accent (#D8A5FF) or orange (#FFA500)
Hardcode colors; always use CSS variables
Apply blur less than 4px or more than 8px
Use heavy shadows (opacity > 85% or blur > 32px)
Mix font families inconsistently
